Connected services and the live connection: `ConnectionCard` and `ConnectionBanner`.

ConnectionCard: `name`, `icon` or `logo` (node, e.g. the service's mark), `text`, `status` ("connected" | "expiring" | "error" | "missing"), `statusLabel`, `action`.
ConnectionBanner: `state` ("reconnecting" | "offline" | "online" hides it), `seconds`, `text`, `onRetry`. Thin bar under the top bar.
