Moderating customer reviews before and after they go live.

Props: `author`, `verified`, `product`, `date`, `rating`, `title`, `text`, `state` ("pending" | "published" | "hidden" | "flagged"), `stateLabel`, `highlight` (words to mark, e.g. refund, scam), `reply`, `onPublish`, `onHide`, `onReply(text)`, `replyLabel`.

- Pending reviews carry the signal edge until someone decides.
- Hiding is reversible; deleting a review is not offered here.
