# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps: being kind and patient, respecting different opinions and experiences, giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback, taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour: sexualised language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, public or private harassment, publishing others' private information without their explicit permission, and other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone represents the project in public. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code and issues that do not follow it, and may ban contributors temporarily or permanently.

Report unacceptable behaviour to **hello@twentyfirst.media**. All reports are reviewed promptly and fairly, and the privacy of the reporter is respected. Enforcement follows the [Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines): correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban.
