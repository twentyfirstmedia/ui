# ResizablePanels

Two or more panes with drag handles between them, side by side or stacked; panels can nest.

- Handles are separators with a value: ←/→ (↑/↓ when stacked) resize by 2%, Home/End jump to the
  limits, double-click resets to equal sizes.
- `min` keeps every pane above a share of the whole; `storageKey` remembers the sizes.
