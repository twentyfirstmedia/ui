# MessagePreview

A message to a customer, or a post, exactly as it goes out: the channel (name and icon; register a brand icon for the logo), the recipient masked (`maskRecipient()`), the sending account, the reply window (open, closing, closed) for channels that only allow free text for a while after the customer's last message, the last messages of the conversation for context, and the text or a pre-approved template with its category and parameters. Posts show their image or video (`media`). Ends with "Goes out once after A".
