# MailFolders

The folder tree of a mailbox: standard folders with their icons (inbox, drafts, sent, scheduled, starred, archive, junk, trash) and your own folders, nested, with unread counts.

Each folder has a menu, from its "…" button, a right click or Shift+F10: a new folder inside, rename and delete for own folders, and "Empty folder" for trash and junk. Deleting and emptying ask first. Renaming and new folders are edited in place (Enter saves, Esc cancels). Folders with `href` render as links (`linkAs`).
