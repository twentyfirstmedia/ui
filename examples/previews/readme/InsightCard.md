# InsightCard

Something the assistant noticed: what, where it comes from, why it is shown and what to do.

- `sources` are chips like the ones under chat answers; `why` folds away behind "Why am I seeing
  this?".
- `onAccept` (with `acceptLabel`), `onSnooze` and `onDismiss` add the actions.
- `tone` sets the accent: info, success, warning or highlight.
