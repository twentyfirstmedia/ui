# Separator, Label and AspectRatio

Small layout primitives.

- **Separator**: a hairline between groups, horizontal or vertical, optionally with a label in the middle ("or").
- **Label**: the field label on its own, with a required mark or a muted hint ("optional").
- **AspectRatio**: a box that keeps its ratio (16 : 9 by default) for images, video and placeholders.
