Consistency pass over every component in both kits and themes. The app shell responds to its own width (container queries); kit boxes no longer depend on the host's box-sizing; controls and code use the kit fonts; bold stays on the type scale; status shapes, list icons, stat values, pagination and invoice numbers line up; segmented controls and tabs no longer stretch or break mid-word. Gauge, Funnel and ProgressRing now fill when reduced motion is on. Pro follows its reference more closely: decorative tints and chart series are monochrome, bars are flat, in-flow cards use hairlines, badges sit on the corner. New: `formatPercent`.
